# Jatam — Landing page image brief & AI generation prompts

Original image prompts for every image slot on the landing page
(`standalone/index.html`). Each entry gives the placement, recommended size, a
text-safe area and an original generation prompt.

---

## Global style lock (append to every prompt)

> Editorial commercial food photography for a premium UK African & Caribbean grocery
> brand. Natural daylight with soft directional fill, rich but realistic colour, deep
> forest-green `#064D2E` and fresh-green `#0DA44A` props with restrained Jatam-red
> `#E53935` accents. Matte natural surfaces, shallow depth of field, ultra-detailed,
> photorealistic, 8k, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no packaging brand names.

### Negative prompt (use on all)

> text, letters, words, logo, watermark, brand marks, cluttered composition,
> plastic-looking food, oversaturated neon, HDR halo, cartoon, illustration, 3D render,
> low resolution, blurry, deformed hands, extra fingers, duplicate products, misspelled
> packaging, purple tint, cold blue tint

## Production notes

- Generate at **2× the target size**, export **WebP** (quality ~80); keep each under
  ~150–250 KB.
- Leave the stated text-safe areas free — add all headlines/CTAs in HTML, not in the
  image (AI text is unreliable).
- Keep the same light direction (soft window light from the left) across the set for a
  cohesive brand feel.
- Once generated, drop the files into `standalone/assets/img/landing/` and wire them into
  the hero, category tiles, banners and country tiles (the existing gradient/emoji can
  remain as a fallback), then rebuild with `node build.mjs`.
